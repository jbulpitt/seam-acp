The FBI/NSA/CISA advisory alleges industrial-scale unauthorized distillation by DeepSeek, Moonshot, Alibaba, MiniMax, StepFun, and Z.ai. Anthropic’s report supplies its own attribution and exchange-volume claims; China calls the accusations groundless and warns of countermeasures. This is a policy-and-training storyline spanning the labs, not evidence that any particular model is derived from another.
